Upload index.html and manage.html to the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository, replacing old files. DO NOT replace calendar.jpg.
Open https://felkowskib.github.io/lhs-sensory-room/manage.html and sign in with the email and password created in Supabase Authentication > Users.
Select dates, click Save, and refresh the public calendar. Your TinyURL remains the same.

IMPORTANT: For a date already paid (e.g. December 1), check it and Save.

The public page refuses to display donation buttons if the database cannot be reached, to avoid double bookings.
The Supabase RLS policies must permit authenticated INSERT and DELETE only for your exact user UID.
