LHS SENSORY ROOM — ONE-CLICK DONATION CALENDAR

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html: public mobile-friendly donation calendar
- calendar.jpg: your original Christmas design
- manage.html: private helper for marking dates TAKEN

PUBLISH FREE USING NETLIFY DROP
1. Extract this ZIP file to a folder.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in if prompted.
3. Drag the extracted LHS_One_Click_Donation_Calendar FOLDER onto the upload area.
4. Netlify gives you a public website address. Test a date on your phone and share that address.

MARK A DATE TAKEN
1. Open your published website address, adding /manage.html at the end.
2. Check all dates that have confirmed donations.
3. Click "Download updated index.html".
4. Replace the index.html file inside your extracted folder with the downloaded one.
5. In Netlify, open your site and use its Deploys page to upload the updated folder again.
6. Check the public site to verify the TAKEN labels appear.
